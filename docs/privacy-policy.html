Privacy Policy — SnapFind AI

Last updated: 14 October 2026

The short version. SnapFind AI finds you in a batch of event photos by matching faces. All of that matching happens on your phone. Your selfie, your event photos, the face data derived from them, and the results are never uploaded, never sent to us, and never sent to anyone else. We do not operate a server that receives your photos. We have no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Who this covers

This policy covers the SnapFind AI Android application. It is written for the people who use the app.

What the app handles

To do its job, the app works with:

  • A selfie you choose from your device.
  • A ZIP archive of event photos you choose from your device.
  • Face data derived from those images. To compare faces, the app detects faces and converts each one into a numeric representation (a "face embedding"). This is biometric data, and we treat it as sensitive.
  • Match results — which photos contained a face matching your selfie, and how close each match was.

You provide the selfie and the archive explicitly, through your device's own file and photo picker. The app does not browse, scan or index your photo library, and it does not request permission to read your photos in bulk.

Where processing happens

Entirely on your device. The face detection and face comparison models run locally using on-device inference. No image, no face embedding, and no match result is transmitted off your device at any point.

We do not receive this data. There is no account to create, nothing to sign in to, and no server that holds your photos.

What does leave your device

Exactly one thing: on first launch, the app downloads the two face-matching model files (about 16 MB total) that make on-device matching possible. These are generic model weights, identical for every user. They are hosted as public release assets on GitHub.

That request contains no personal data — no photos, no face data, no identifiers created by the app. As with any download, GitHub as the host may log standard technical information such as your IP address and the time of the request, under GitHub's own privacy statement. We never see that information.

After that one-time download the app needs no network access to match faces, and will work offline.

What is stored on your device

WhatWhereHow long
The two face-matching model files App-private storage Downloaded once and kept, so first run is not repeated
Photos that matched your selfie App-private storage, one folder per job Until you remove that job's matches or delete the job
Working files for a run in progress (your selfie, the archive, matched photos) App-private storage, one folder per run Deleted when the run ends; leftovers are cleaned up automatically on app start
Job records: timestamps, match counts, match scores, file paths App-private database. Never image data. Until you delete the job, or clear the app's data
Photos you chose to download Your gallery, under Pictures/SnapFindAI/ Yours. They remain even if you uninstall the app

App-private storage is a location only SnapFind AI can read. Other apps cannot access it.

Photos you explicitly download are deliberately placed in your normal gallery so that your photo apps can see them, and are deliberately left behind on uninstall, because at that point they are your photos rather than the app's.

Cloud backup is switched off

Android can automatically back up an app's data to your Google account. SnapFind AI disables this, and every storage location listed above is explicitly excluded from backup.

This is deliberate. The app's central promise is that your photos stay on your phone, and that promise has to include Google's cloud backup. Nothing is lost by switching it off: the models can be downloaded again, and a job can be re-run.

Permissions, and why each exists

PermissionWhy
Internet To download the two model files on first launch. Nothing else.
Notifications To show matching progress and completion, and download status. If you deny it, matching still works; you simply will not see the notification.
Foreground service (data sync) A matching run can take several minutes. This lets it keep running when you leave the app, instead of being killed and losing your progress.
Storage write (Android 9 and older only) Needed only on older Android versions to save a photo into your gallery. On Android 10 and newer it is not requested at all.

The app does not request broad access to your photo library, your camera, your location, your contacts, or your microphone.

Who we share data with

No one. There are no advertising networks, no analytics or crash-reporting services, no social media integrations, and no data brokers. We have no server receiving your data, so there is nothing for us to share, sell or lose.

Your choices and control

  • Delete a single job — long-press a job in Recent Jobs and delete it. This removes its matched photos and its database records.
  • Remove matched photos — remove them individually or in bulk from the results screen.
  • Delete everything the app holds — clear the app's data in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Both remove all of the app-private storage above.
  • Photos you downloaded stay in Pictures/SnapFindAI/ and are managed by you, in your own gallery app, like any other photo.

Because nothing is ever uploaded, there is no server-side copy to request, export or erase. Deleting locally is genuinely deletion.

Face and biometric data

Face embeddings generated by the app are biometric data. They are computed on your device for the sole purpose of comparing the faces you asked to be compared, are stored only in app-private storage, are never transmitted, and are removed when you delete the job they belong to or clear the app's data.

They are never used to identify you to us or to anyone else, never used for training, and never combined with any other data source.

Please only use the app with photographs you are entitled to use. If an archive contains photographs of other people, you are responsible for having an appropriate basis to process them in the place where you live.

Children

SnapFind AI is not directed at children and is not intended for use by children. We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone — including children — because the app does not collect data at all.
